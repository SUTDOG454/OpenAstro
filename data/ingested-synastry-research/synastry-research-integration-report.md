# Synastry Research Source Integration Report

**Status:** Integrated with research boundaries  
**Generated:** 2026-08-17

## Scope

Twenty-nine user-supplied synastry CSV and Python-template files were preserved, hashed, classified, normalized into source-profile records, and linked to the unified master. The ingestion is limited to provenance, schema, static-code, and source-claim handling. It does not calculate a chart, execute a scoring engine, validate a statistical effect, train a model, generate a compatibility score, or provide relationship advice.

## Artifact Classification

| Family | Sources | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Template or mock methodology | 6 | Retained as methodology-bound examples and marked pending review. |
| Untrusted scoring-code templates | 5 | Statically inventoried only; not executed, dependency-resolved, security-reviewed, or adopted. |
| Source-reported statistical outputs | 11 | Preserved as source claims, not independently replicated validation evidence. |
| Small named synastry-cohort candidates | 5 | Retained as possible exploratory source cohorts with provenance and sampling gaps. |
| Other source tables | 2 | Preserved as source tables pending schema and methodology review. |

## Integration Result

The unified master now contains `synastry_research_layer` and `research_layer.synastry_candidate_material` references. These pointers expose the source inventory, normalized records, claim register, discrepancy register, validation report, and integration report while retaining the boundary that canonical registries and standard delineation/calculation engines are unchanged.

The source-reported statistic register has eleven entries. Their p-values, effect sizes, intervals, correlations, and score summaries are explicitly `unverified_pending_recalculation`: the package does not contain a frozen raw cohort, a documented sampling frame, pre-registration, a reproducible analysis environment, or an independent rerun.

## Boundaries and Limitations

> Templates, mock tables, source-reported outputs, small named-pair cohorts, and uploaded scoring code are not empirical replication evidence or a production compatibility system.

All source rights remain pending review. The small named-pair candidates require a documented sampling frame, inclusion and exclusion criteria, birth-data provenance and Rodden ratings, relationship-outcome coding, consent or public-data basis, duplicate policy, and missingness policy before use in any research dataset. Source files with fewer than thirty rows are treated as fixtures or exploratory tables only, not standalone generalizable training or confirmatory validation data.

No source-provided aspect weights, orb settings, harmonic rules, labels, relationship outcomes, or score thresholds were added to canonical registries. No synastry or relationship prediction, rating, personal decision support, or causal conclusion was created.

## Required Review Before Empirical Use

A future empirical synastry study must begin with a registered hypothesis, explicit cohort construction rules, resolved data rights, raw chart-data provenance, deterministic and versioned calculation settings, an independently coded outcome, a frozen data package, a holdout or time-aware split where temporal ordering applies, multiple-testing controls, uncertainty reporting, and independent review. Until then, this material remains source-derived or methodology-bound research context only.
