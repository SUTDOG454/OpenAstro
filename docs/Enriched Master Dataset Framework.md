These asteroids symbolize the "Web of Wyrd" and the duration of fated soul contracts.
Klotho (97) - The Spinner: Represents beginnings, births, ignition of processes, and initial conditions; reveals what an individual is "made of" and the preliminary understanding of their path.

Atropos (273) - The Cutter: Known as "the Inflexible One," she governs endings, finality, and the manner of death or transformation; her placement marks where a thread is irrevocably cut.
